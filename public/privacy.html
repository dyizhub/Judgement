Privacy Policy — Judgement

Last updated: 2026-07-27

Judgement is a multiplayer card game. This policy describes what information the app and its server handle.

What we collect

When you create or join a room, you enter a display name. That name is sent to the game server and shown to the other players in your room for the duration of the game. It is not stored after the room ends, is not linked to any account or device identity, and is never shared with any third party.

There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no email addresses, and no persistent user profiles anywhere in this app.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No sale or sharing of any data with third parties
  • No location data
  • No access to contacts, photos, camera, or microphone

Server logs

Like any web server, our hosting provider processes standard connection information (such as IP address) transiently to deliver the service and keep it secure. This is not used to identify individual players and is not retained beyond routine operational logging.

Children's privacy

Judgement does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game contains no chat, no user accounts, and no way to contact other players outside of the game itself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
